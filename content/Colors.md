##### Colors
- The color palette should be layers of white, off white, and natural colors (wood, terracotta, etc.) with color coming from dried and fresh flowers, rotating paintings, gourds, or the like.
- With our colors, we want to communicate texture, materiality, and patina digitally. 
- Botanical and natural colors
- In the restaurant, color should come from paintings, driec flowers, vegetables. Colors should only come from things which are endogenous to the process. 

Colors show care. Fresh picked flowers. fresh vegetables. The wood, stone, earth colors are of unchanging materials. Colors are what change, and this show the care of the operator, that they are engaged in the ritual of change as the seasons are. 

**The color scheme that stands out in these images, which I quite enjoy, seems to be shades off white/grey/beige/browns + shades of pastel-y blues/greens/jade + few pops of orange/red**
