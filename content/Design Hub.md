##### What is an aesthetic?

It encompasses near everything. The material choices. The atomic choices, like which glass to get.  

How employees move through the space, place down a dish, how a chair feels to sit in, how a door feels to open, how a conversation unfolds, how we speak, the sounds, how long it takes from one dish to the next. Performance is an aesthetic, which is partly why excellence, everywhere, matters. An aesthetic experience is partly closed when any thing goes awry. Each of these things generate an immediate aesthetic response, creates a collective response, and is part of a whole unified, or dis-unified, aesthetic.

This document should concern itself, principally, with informing material considerations and the Garnet universe that one steps into. But, it should also be referenced to with respecft to oral communication, toilet paper, acoustics, and more

"The details are not the details, they make the design." [[Charles Eames]]

There should be continuity across branding, experience, interiors, socials, website, language, etc... KNOWING [[Words that describe the space]], serve as scaffolding across everything.
