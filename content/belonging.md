[[From Taste to Belonging]]

best way to connect with place is food - farmers, region, craft. 



- The act of tasting coffee is not about “this coffee tastes like X,” where X is a flavor note like blackcurrant, hibiscus, jasmine, but more “this flavor/sensation, X, which I am experiencing tells me Y about how/where/by whom the wine was made.” from my friend Adam
- When you interpret a sensation, you are drawing a relation... between taste and region, acidity and altitude, texture and craft, and so on.
- Every drink, experience is unique… attention is how we understand that uniqueness, the slow unveiling of a world unto itself, etc.. We begin with coffee, and use that practice of noticing as a matrix for relating generally - we we learn to attend – with care, slowness, without needing to change – we understand the basic pattern of humanity

- **Knowledge**
    
    Knowledge is the relational link between experience and origin. Drawing relations forms the basis of sentiment (the feeling of connection), which informs ethics, which is the way we choose to relate, care, etc... Knowledge transforms experience into a carrier of sentiment.
    
    If we attend to coffee, and creates the links (for example, between taste and craft), it develops our sentiment...
    
    Here, we can think of sentiment as the felt reality of a relation (i.e. knowledge). Belonging stems from feeling the significance of our ties to others. Sentiment, then, is the affective bridge that links knowledge to belonging.
    
- Craft
    
    - is a form of care, when we do something with care we create space for relationship to develop
        
        Craft is where making and knowing come together ([[richard sennett]]). When we grind, brew, and serve coffee, that is a condensing experience into an act of care.
        
        The receiver, in attending to the act, relates taste to craft, and thus experiences that form of care in the totality of the experience – taste, warmth, materiality of the cup, etc..
        
        This is where attention becomes the primary interface for the development of taste – taste being the discernment of craft, of something made well.
        
        Our epistemic frame joins experience with craft, which then embeds the signifance of beauty and care into experience.

- [[Stephen Dedalus]] in [[James Joyce]] [[Book - A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man]], we see this attempt at belonging made through organization of relations.
- Attention organizes objects and experience by relating them. We understand things relationally; that is by how they exist relative to another thing.
- It is a mistake to think of loneliness as a simply a lack of important human relations. Loneliness is most a problem of belonging, which is both the lack of self-to-other and other-other relations AND the lack of felt significance of those relations. When we draw a relation, which is to be done through attention, we're in a sense developing our co-existence with an other, or moving from aloneness to oneness ([[Erich Fromm]]). It is not only about inserting oneself into a network of relations, but inhabiting a world of known and significant relations.
