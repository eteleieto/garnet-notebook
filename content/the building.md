

Riverside was tenement housing, first public housing in the US. 
- brickwork on the 4th fl, the 4 leaf clover stamps in the black metal
- black wrought iron with **quatrefoils**
- terra cotta, brick work for texture
- social reformer - continuing with that hope, a place for people of all types to meet and commune
	- important that it is approachable for everyone, AND that it is designed for emergence
- these streets are remarkable. They show a diversity in architectural activity and population, and an ambition in the built environment that for a quiet neighborhood is magical. 
- arts and crafts feel
- 280 apts and 19 retails originally, 49% of the site for green courtyard space
- brilliant job in taking a large structure and breaking up the facade to be charming and intimate, without any fancy materials, and a simple palate of choices.
