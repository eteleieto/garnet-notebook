We drove up one weekend to Ian Ingersoll in West Cornwall CT, custom furniture makers known for their shaker work. Their work was gorgeous, across tables, chairs, bedframes and the like. They make reproduction ladderback chairs true to their original form. 

Also a few problems here.

The four slat ladderback chair felt both too stiff and too grand, too imposing and inflexible. There was the two-slat option, which had a more playful profile, but these didn't provide sufficient lumbar support. More generally, the restaurant isn't endeavoring to be a Shaker restaurant, and these chairs felt too directly representative of the shakers.

"A post and rung chair is a stick chair with a fiber seat. It has cylindrical mortises bored into vertical posts to receive the ends of horizontal rungs. When the chair is first assembled, the posts contain more moisture than the rungs. The chair is held together by the shrinking of the posts around the dry rungs. It is one of the few things made today that depends on the shrinking actions of wood." From her Book
