Lunch at Garnet is kind of like a canteen. We have a small selection of offerings, which typically consist of a plate for lunch, a snack-size plate for noshing, an assortment of treats at the bar, and a rotating selection of drinks. We'll also offer specials, most of which are served family style (see [[serve billiards for lunch]])

You order, pick-up, and return your dishes at the bar. 

We always have a drink option that is $3, typically a tea. 

Lunch changes most days.
