When you walk down the street, within your visual field are two kinds of building frontage: homes and businesses. There are others too, but these form the basic typology of the city. On this imaginary street, all the residences within view are inaccessible. You cannot, reasonably, enter them. You can see the apartment, but it affords you no action. 

And then there are storefronts. Some vie for your attention and bank account. They put up signs, their doors are left open. The storefronts are porous – in the city, they make up our field of action.

Activity takes place elsewhere in the city too, but in more curated ways away from the street.
