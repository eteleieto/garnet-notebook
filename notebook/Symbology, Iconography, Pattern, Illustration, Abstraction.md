Take the earthly and organic, and abstract it, through analog forms.

For example, take peas and abstract them into a stamp, woodcut, or woven pattern. 

This is what the restaurant is already doing – taking produce and highlighting it through hands-on abstraction (cooking). 

Environmentally natural objects, like flowers, vegetables, leaves, and grains, or place-based objects like maps or buildings are both fine references.
