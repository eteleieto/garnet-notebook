**What is the notebook for?**

[[Jane Jacobs]] takes the shopkeeper to be the most intelligent person on the street. 

What kind of knowledge is this?

They see and know the problems, in their space, in their building, on the block, and in the neighborhood. They know the afflictions of other businesses and their triumphs. The bureacsy of the city. 

If this person takes in a ton of information about the block, about the functioning, or lack their of, 

few parts
- to have a place to turn to for thinking
- to document the life of this restaurant, on this block, in this neighborhood
	- Menus and philosophy and dishes, etc.. so that someone may go further into any part of the restaurant which they wish
	- to build up a living archive, so that it may last, as many analog projects do not, for longer
- to documents the patterns of life that exist within and around the restaurant. to treat the storefront as a form worth studying
- to document and open source the component parts of this restaurant, so that others may replicate it
