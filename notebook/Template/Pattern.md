# Shared geography creates permission for familiarity

## Situation
When people who do not know one another gather in a neighborhood place.

## Tension
People may recognize one another as local without having a legitimate
reason to initiate conversation.

## Pattern
Make shared geography visible enough that it becomes a socially acceptable
basis for recognition and conversation.
