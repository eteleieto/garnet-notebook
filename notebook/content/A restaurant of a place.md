##### What does it mean for a restaurant to be truly of a place?

For one, it means of a people who are related to that place, who know a place beyond words which can be attributed to it. I grew up in Brooklyn Heights, and feel eternally grateful for having grown up here.

More than the people, it also means food, materials, handcrafts, and aesthetics of this place. There are pre-existing aesthetic traditions, and so there are many nested within each other.

One cannot engage in a place-based restaurant, in my view, and eat separate from all others. To be "of a place" is to recognize a fundamental connection with. How odd it seems to connect with food of a place, craft of a place, etc... and yet to be disconnected with all others in that room. Eating with others, then, is not an atomic preference, but a necessary condition as part of a unified philosophy.

##### What is "the place", as meant here?

It is a many things, not a singular place. It is the people, the space, the building and the street. It is Willowtown, and Brooklyn Heights, and everyone who designates the place within sub mileage of their home. It is Brooklyn and NYC, and it is the Northeast United States. This "place" is concoction of places, for which each person engages in their own designation.
