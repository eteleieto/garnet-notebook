##### **Dining to Kitchen Continuity**
The space will feature an open kitchen, and modular tables that can be adjusted for both communal use and small groups/1-1. The kitchen and dining room should have a continuity, such that the sitting area feels like a dinner party at a friend's apartment. Kitchen storage – ferments, pickles, preserves, etc.. – should be either within the dining room or at the least visible from the dining room
