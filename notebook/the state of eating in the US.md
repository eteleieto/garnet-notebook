"In 2024, almost three of every four restaurant orders were not eaten in a restaurant, according to data from the National Restaurant Association." ([Source](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/01/30/dining/food-delivery-apps-doordash-uber.html))

This is true for restaurants... as our restaurants become, in effect, ghost kitchens. In Prospect Heights, restaurants on Vanderbilt were churning out takeout orders. The margins are slimmer, so they have to sell more too. Inside, hospitality feels stunted. 

More, solo-dining up, so restaurants have to accommodate with more single tables, and thus less total seats.
