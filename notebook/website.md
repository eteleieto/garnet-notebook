### Food

Putnam, neighbors strongest sign of flourishing neighborhood. Gathering at the table is the best way to get to know a person. knowing the food of ones region is the best way to feel connected to ones place. 
- food as insight into temporality of place, people, land, flavors, etc.. 

Doing nothing new. We are a restaurant of this place. We serve food of this region, support heritage craftspersons of this region. We support relations in our neighborhood. 

We're a neighborhood restaurant. The restaurant is built by friends and craftspersons of this region. We use only 100% ingredients from the Northeastern U.S. It is a restaurant truly of this place. 

We listen to farmers and producers about what grows well here, and what has grown well here historically. We're especially excited about working with ingredients that support these farmers and the land. We serve plants. 

We take care with everything we do. 

We treat ingredients simply and with reverence. Everything we make by hand. 

We ferment and preserve to extend the seasons, to explore deliciousness, and to reduce waste. 

We don't serve a fixed menu. It changes weekly, and sometimes daily. But, our food is specific. It is of a time, a place, a people. 

We cook what's in peak form, inspired by our farmers and the land. We forage sometimes too.
